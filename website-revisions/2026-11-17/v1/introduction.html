Trustworthy & efficient AI

I study how learning systems can make reliable decisions with limited data, computation, and energy. My research connects the statistical foundations of graph learning with efficient neural architectures, adaptive inference, and generative methods for autonomous systems.

My work spans graph neural networks, efficient language-model reasoning, wireless networks, and UAV autonomy. I combine mathematical analysis with computational evaluation to understand when a method works, where it fails, and what it costs to deploy.

I am Co-PI of the NSF-funded AERIAL project (2025–2028; total award $599,961). Previously, I held research and teaching appointments at UMass Amherst. I earned my Ph.D. in Data Science from Worcester Polytechnic Institute in 2023.
