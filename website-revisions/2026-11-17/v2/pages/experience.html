Research that connects theory, algorithms, and implementation

My work combines mathematical analysis with computational experimentation. I focus on the constraints that shape useful AI systems: limited training data, inference cost, model size, robustness, and the physical requirements of networked systems.

Applied research areas

Efficient inference and compact models

Adaptive stopping for LLM self-consistency, compact graph neural networks for radio resource allocation, and constrained low-rank flow-matching models for UAV coverage. The central question is how to reduce computation while preserving the quality of a decision or trajectory.

Reliable learning for networked systems

Graph learning for wireless resource management and autonomous systems, with research on structural limits, adversarial attacks, and robust decision-making. I connect theoretical guarantees to explicit assumptions and reproducible computational evaluation.
