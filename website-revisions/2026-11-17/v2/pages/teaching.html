From mathematical foundations to working systems

Selected course experiences

Machine Learning for Engineers: connect regression and classification to neural networks, GNNs, LLMs, and reinforcement learning. Efficient-ML topics include quantization, pruning, neural architecture search, and resource-constrained edge applications. Engineering projects give students practice choosing models and testing their assumptions.

Digital Image Processing: move from sampling and quantization through enhancement, restoration, segmentation, and recognition. Computational projects connect filtering and deconvolution to the practical problem of recovering and interpreting imperfect images.

Professional judgment: make assumptions explicit, establish meaningful baselines, document experiments, and examine accuracy, reliability, and cost together. The aim is reproducible engineering practice supported by mathematical reasoning.
